## Limit on the review record

This qualifies round_4_limitations[10], which says a registered review cannot be dropped. That holds only for commits the freezing repository can still read. A later review's row and report that were never pushed can be erased there by a reset, an expired reflog and a gc, using no fabricated record, and no check at the freeze then sees them, whether or not the study mirror (javrodriguez/gars-cloud-mirror) is a remote. With the mirror as a remote, the freeze binds HEAD only to the freeze/r4-20261021 tip as last fetched: a mirror rewritten by a force-push and then fetched passes, and commits on the mirror outside that line are not scanned for review rows or reports. So the statement that the freeze seeded from the last blind review rests on the mirror's push records for what was pushed, and on the operator's word for what was not.
