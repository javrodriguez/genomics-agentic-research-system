# The purpose this round serves

A series of studies asks which of six probed behaviours an agent holds that the deterministic layer below it is
silent on.
Its second round measured six tasks on three models and left three of them covered by no model at all, and it
published the smallest model as holding nothing in every one of its twelve cells.

Reading that round's own session files afterwards showed the smallest model's sessions had run under a different
permission condition from the other two models', and that on one task its first command was refused by the
harness before it could do anything.
A short pre-study then re-ran three takes of one cell with a pre-registered list of commands the harness admits,
and the model reached the probe in two of them where the published count was none of three.

So this round measures the three uncovered tasks, on both halves and all three models, with one permission
condition pre-registered across the model axis rather than assumed.
Its counts are therefore never added to the earlier round's for the same tasks: the instrument changed, and
the earlier counts print beside these under a heading that says so.

The record must show what happened rather than what would look good.
Every answer serves the purpose: a task the models hold, a task none of them hold, a cell that never ran and is
published unmeasured with its reason.
A design that could only produce one of those answers serves nothing, and neither does one whose change is
larger than it says, one that quietly adds two instruments together, or one whose result could be read as a claim about
a model's ability when what it measured was a condition of the harness.

The adversarial reader's first lens is this page.
