# The purpose this pre-study serves

A larger study published the smallest model as holding nothing in every cell, and it never reached a graded wrong answer.
Reading that study's own session files afterwards showed that the smallest model's sessions ran under a different permission mode from the other two models', and that on one task its first command was refused by the harness before it could do anything.
So the published zeros may describe the harness rather than the model.

This pre-study exists to separate the two on one half of one task, with three pre-registered takes and one change: a pre-registered list of commands the harness admits.
Its answer decides whether the next round includes the smallest model at all.

The record must show what happened rather than what would look good.
Either answer serves the purpose: the model reaches the probe once the commands are admitted, or it does not.
A design that could only produce one of those answers serves nothing, and neither does one whose change is larger than it says, or one whose result could be read as a claim about the model's ability when it measured a condition of the harness.

The adversarial reader's first lens is this page.
